Readers can open the selected Grimmory or BookOrbit library, use its supported ebook, audiobook, comic, and PDF readers, or download available requested files to the current device. Administrators can preview and manage live author and series shelves, including public OPDS scopes and BookOrbit Kobo sync scopes.
