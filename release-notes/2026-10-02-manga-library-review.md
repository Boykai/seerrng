Administrators can review manga library matches through the API under `/api/v1/manga/library`: list unmatched manga and their proposals, confirm a proposal, match a manga by hand, or reject a title so the scan never proposes or matches it for that manga again. Proposals are still never confirmed automatically, and decisions never change Suwayomi.
