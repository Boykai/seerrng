Approved manga requests are now sent to the connected Suwayomi server: SeerrNG adds the manga to the library and a SeerrNG category, chooses the requested chapters, and queues their downloads. A new Manga Dispatch Sweep job sends waiting requests every 5 minutes. SeerrNG never deletes files, removes library entries, or changes Suwayomi's settings.
