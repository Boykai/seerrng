The manga Discover page can now include or exclude genres and tags and filter by format, status, country, source material, start year, AniList score, chapters and volumes. Titles can also be sorted by start date or title, and sorts other than Trending can be reversed. Filters stay in the page address, and adult genres and tags appear only while adult manga is allowed.
