Manga details pages list a title's chapters while a Suwayomi server is configured, with each chapter's number, name, date and whether it is downloaded, requested or not requested. Downloaded chapters that a request already offers you as a Download copy link to that download. Viewing the list never makes Suwayomi contact a source.
