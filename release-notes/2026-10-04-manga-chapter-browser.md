Manga details pages list a title's chapters while a Suwayomi server is configured, with each chapter's number, name, date and whether it is downloaded, requested or not requested. Downloaded chapters that a request offers you link to their Download copy. Viewing the list never makes Suwayomi contact a source. On narrow screens, paged lists move their page controls onto separate rows.
