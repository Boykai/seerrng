Manga cards can now show their titles always or on hover: use Title View on the manga Discover page or Manga under Card Titles in General settings. Genres and tags on manga details pages link to matching Discover results, and the genre and tag menus widen for long names. The Release Calendar counts each manga chapter number once, on its earliest release that Suwayomi still lists.
