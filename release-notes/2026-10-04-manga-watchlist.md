Manga can now be added to your SeerrNG watchlist from a manga card or details page. With the new Auto-Request Manga permission and profile setting, watchlisted manga are requested through the normal manga request flow. AniList users can also opt in to adding their Planning manga to the watchlist; SeerrNG only reads that list.
