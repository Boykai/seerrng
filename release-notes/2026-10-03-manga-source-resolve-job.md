The new Manga Source Resolve job matches requested manga to your selected Suwayomi sources every 10 minutes. It matches a title by itself only through an exact MangaDex link; other results wait for an administrator. It sends requested titles to MangaDex's title search, and Suwayomi searches the selected sources and stores what it finds.
