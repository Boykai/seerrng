Administrators can remove a title's Suwayomi match under Settings → Manga Sources, whose new Bound filter lists matched titles. Manga requests flagged as needing attention now count under Needs Attention. Manga details pages list open issues, narrow screens keep chapter Status and downloads in view, and profile pages load manga request cards with their request list.
