The Manga Library Scan also matches manga through their MyAnimeList tracking and MangaDex links, and proposes an AniList title for the rest, for later review. For this it sends MyAnimeList IDs and titles of unmatched manga to AniList, and MangaDex UUIDs to MangaDex, at most 10 requests of each kind per run.
